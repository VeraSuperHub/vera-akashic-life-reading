# Akashic Style Patterns

Use these patterns only after chart facts have been calculated or supplied by a trusted source and the user confirms the chart. These notes are intentionally anonymized and must not store names, exact birth data, locations, or biographical details from prior readings.

## Primary Principle: Cross-System Correction

The strongest readings come from correction, not decoration. Before writing the narrative, ask:

- Does one system seem to show absence while another shows analogous strength?
- Does one system describe core temperament while another describes the public mask?
- Does a branch structure point to one element or life rhythm while house rulership points to where it must be expressed?
- Does timing from BaZi luck cycles, annual themes, or dasha describe background weather rather than an event guarantee?

When a correction appears, make it the thesis, but keep the systems distinct. Western fire/earth/water/air and BaZi wood/fire/earth/metal/water are not the same technical elements. For example: "BaZi Fire phase is not visible, but astrology shows strong fire-like expression signatures; read this as an access/release pattern, not as astrology replacing BaZi Fire phase" is stronger and more accurate than "you need more fire."

## Body-Response Practice

For `akashic-simple` readings, include body-response prompting as a validation layer:

1. Opening invitation: ask the user to notice where the body tightens, warms, relaxes, or becomes emotional.
2. Mid-reading check: after the core thesis or a major archetype, invite them to pause and notice which phrase has physical charge.
3. Closing invitation: ask them to return to the one paragraph their body reacted to most; that is the best refinement entry point.

Do not present body response as proof. Treat it as the user's own resonance signal.

## Free One-Record Output

Use this shape when the user asks for Akashic record, past-life and this-life reading, soul record, free reading, short reading, or life-purpose reading:

1. Chart Anchor: one compact list of verified systems and uncertainty flags.
2. Core Thesis: one cross-system correction or synthesis.
3. One Akashic Record: one symbolic scene, not a catalogue of past lives.
4. One Mission: one this-life mission sentence.
5. Core Blocker: one recurring pattern that blocks the mission.
6. Next Practice: one grounded practice for the next 7-30 days.
7. Reflection Prompt: one question that helps the user notice resonance or mismatch.

The free version should feel clear, memorable, and shareable. It should not feel like a full paid report.

## Integration Pattern

1. Start with a compact chart anchor: confirmed BaZi, key BaZi structures, verified auxiliary facts such as Tai Yuan when available, modern astrology settings, traditional Western settings, Zi Wei settings, Vedic settings, and optional numerology numbers.
2. Name any boundary-sensitive facts before the thesis: near-hour-boundary birth times, Tai Yuan adjustment choice, sign cusps, house cusps, or school-specific settings.
3. State one cross-system correction thesis in symbolic language, such as "deep storage becomes visible creation" or "inner depth needs public form."
4. Use BaZi mechanics for energy structure: day master, season, branch combinations, hidden stems, ten-god emphasis, auxiliary pillars, luck cycles, and annual themes when verified.
5. Use Zi Wei as a palace-and-star layer when available: life/body palaces, main stars, transformations, decade cycles, annual themes, and repeated palace emphasis.
6. Use Western and Vedic astrology as analogical correction layers: dignity, house rulership, aspects, lagna, nakshatra, and dasha can change the narrative from deficiency to containment, from fear to pacing, or from expression to distribution without claiming the systems calculate the same element.
7. Use traditional Western astrology as a condition-and-timing layer: sect, angularity, traditional rulership, Fortune/Spirit lots, and annual profections can sharpen where the pattern becomes visible, embodied, intentional, or time-activated.
8. Use numerology only as an optional supplemental layer: Life Path for purpose vector, Birthday Number for outer role, and tension/confirmation against chart mechanics.
9. Translate mechanics into one mission, one blocker, and one immediate practice.
10. Close with one reflection question. Prefer a question that invites feedback without opening a full second report.

## Allowed Language

- In `reflective` mode, use "can be read as", "suggests", "symbolically points to", and "a useful practice is".
- In `akashic-simple` mode, use direct address, vivid images, and stronger thesis statements after the symbolic disclaimer.
- It is acceptable to use images such as water, forest, fire, gate, lock, bridge, mountain, archive, field, and vessel when they come from verified chart patterns.
- It is acceptable to discuss past lives or Akashic records as archetypal narrative, but label it as symbolic exploration rather than factual memory.
- It is acceptable to use numerology when the user asks for it, supplies it, or requests a multi-system reading where it adds a distinct lens.

## Avoid

- Do not claim guaranteed past-life events, trauma, illness, marriage outcomes, pregnancy, death, or financial events.
- Do not produce multiple Akashic records, multiple past-life scenes, or multiple missions in the free/simple version.
- Do not say a placement "proves" a hidden history. Say it "supports an archetypal reading" or "offers a symbolic lens."
- Do not make the reading dependent on an unverified calculation, especially disputed auxiliary pillars or house-sensitive astrology points.
- Do not copy a prior reading's person's name, exact birth data, location, or identifying story into examples.
